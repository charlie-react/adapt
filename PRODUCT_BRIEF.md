# ADAPT — Learning that meets you where you are

**Current working MVP · Moonshot by TechCabal 2026 · Lagos, 28–29 October**

ADAPT helps a student access and engage with learning material in a way that works for them, because conventional formats can create reading, visual, hearing or motor-accessibility barriers.

## The learner and their problem

The target learner is a student, Ayodeji, who struggles to access conventional learning materials because of a reading, visual, hearing or motor-accessibility barrier. They need access to the same concepts and examples through a more usable presentation or interaction. ADAPT asks what support helps, not for a disability diagnosis.

**Concrete example:** a student has a five-minute recording of a lesson. Instead of repeatedly replaying it to find important information, they use ADAPT to turn the spoken lesson into structured learning text, preserving its original meaning and key details. They can then navigate the content at their own pace.

## What ADAPT does

**Provide material → choose how you need to learn → receive an accessible learning experience.** The current MVP accepts PDF, text, PNG/JPEG and MP3/WAV. It offers clearer explanations, step-by-step organisation, visual descriptions where supported, and readable spoken content. Read Aloud, supported-browser voice input and optional, low-pressure practice complement the lesson. Learning objectives are optional.

**ADAPT changes how learning happens, not what the learner is expected to learn.** Source preservation is the design principle, not a blanket guarantee of AI accuracy.

## Why now

Learning materials are becoming increasingly digital, but accessibility still isn't built into how many students learn.
AI now makes it possible to adapt learning experiences to individual needs in seconds, rather than requiring every learner to fit one format.
With the right technology, accessibility can go beyond transcription to make existing educational content easier to read, navigate and hear.
ADAPT brings this possibility closer to learners who need a different way to access the same knowledge.

## The demo wow moment

**Watch a five-minute lecture become accessible learning content in about 10 seconds. Then watch the learner navigate the result.** Show the structured sections and preserved key details. Note: the approximate timing is an observed manual result, not a guarantee for every recording.

## What we will not build for this MVP

ADAPT will not replace teachers, courses, learning management systems, or existing educational platforms.
We will not create new curricula or change the original meaning, facts, examples, or learning objectives of source materials.
We will not build a social network, student management system, or permanent library of uploaded materials in this MVP.
We will not attempt to support every disability, file format, language, or learning scenario at launch. We will focus on reliable, accessible core experiences.
